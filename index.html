If someone gets an electric shock
Do NOT touch the person while they may still be in contact with the current.
  1. Switch off the main switch / MCB if you can reach it safely.
  2. If you cannot, push the person away using something dry and non-conductive (dry wooden stick, dry rope). Stand on dry ground or a rubber mat.
  3. Call 112 or 102 immediately.
  4. If the person is not breathing, start CPR if you are trained, and continue until help arrives.
  5. Cover burns loosely with a clean dry cloth. Do not apply ointment or ice.
  6. Every shock victim should be seen by a doctor, even if they feel fine.
Electrical fire
  1. Switch off power at the main if safe.
  2. Never use water. Use a CO₂ or dry powder extinguisher.
  3. Leave the building and call 101.

Emergency reminder

If there is smoke, fire, sparking, or an exposed live wire, move away and keep others away. Contact the electricity utility or emergency services. Do not use this app as an emergency dispatch service.

Electrical safety

⚠️
Exposed wires or sparking

Keep everyone away. Do not touch, move, or try to repair the wire. Contact the electricity utility or a qualified electrician.

💧
Water near electricity

Do not step into standing water near electrical equipment or touch switches with wet hands. Keep away and seek professional help.

🔥
Burning smell or smoke

Move away from the equipment. If safe to do so without approaching danger, alert others and get help. Never pour water on electrical equipment.

🔌
Damaged plug or socket

Stop using it and ask a qualified electrician to inspect it. Do not open the socket yourself.

Important

VoltSathi provides general information only. It cannot assess an emergency remotely or replace a trained electrician.
